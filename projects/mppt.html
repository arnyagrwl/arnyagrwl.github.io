Solar Car MPPT Controller

STM32G474 HRTIM 4-channel synchronous boost Incremental Conductance 1 kW

A 1 kW maximum power point tracking system designed to extract maximum available power from the car's solar array under changing conditions.

Overview

The problem

Solar panels have a nonlinear power curve that shifts with irradiance and temperature. Without active tracking, the array operates off its optimal point and the car loses usable power — MPPT continuously adjusts the operating point to stay at maximum output.

P-V curve / MPP tracking illustration

Power stage

The converter uses a 4-channel synchronous boost topology rated for 1 kW, splitting the array into independently tracked channels so partial shading on one section doesn't drag down the whole array's output.

Power stage schematic

Control

An STM32G474 drives the four channels using its HRTIM peripheral for high-resolution PWM generation. The Incremental Conductance algorithm sets the duty cycle each cycle by comparing instantaneous and incremental conductance to find the maximum power point.

  • High-resolution PWM via HRTIM for tight duty-cycle control
  • Per-channel current/voltage sensing feeding the MPPT algorithm
  • Incremental Conductance tracking, chosen for its accuracy under rapidly changing irradiance
