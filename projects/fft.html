Overview

Why build an FFT in hardware

The FFT is usually taught as software or math on paper. Implementing it as a pipelined hardware block meant actually reasoning about butterfly stages, bit-reversal, and fixed-point precision as physical resources and timing — not abstract operations.

Butterfly stage diagram

Architecture

  • 64-point radix-2 decimation-in-time (DIT) FFT
  • Fully pipelined across the six butterfly stages for continuous throughput
  • Q15 fixed-point arithmetic to keep the design hardware-efficient
